Fretwise

Learn the theory inside your tabs

Paste any guitar tab. Fretwise finds its key and chords, writes them as numerals (I, IV, V, vi…) and labels every note, so you see why the tab works, not just where your fingers go.

Colors = scale degrees. Filled = in the chord, dashed = melody/passing note. Hover a note to see what it is, click to hear it.

    Import a tab

    From a tab website: drag this button to your bookmarks bar once → → Fretwise
    1. Open a tab page (any site) and click → Fretwise in your bookmarks bar. It copies the tab.
    2. Come back here and click Paste.

    Or paste a normal 6-line ASCII tab (e|---0---|) yourself. If there's a line of chord names right above the tab lines, those chords are used. Otherwise the app guesses them from the notes. Rhythm is ignored.

    Imported tabs are saved in this browser.
